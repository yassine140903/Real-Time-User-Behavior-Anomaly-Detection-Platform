\chapter{Enriched Feature Schema}
\label{app:features}

The 30 features below are computed by the Enrichment Service for every transaction event and constitute the input vector for both the Autoencoder and the LSTM.

1 & \code{amount} & Raw event & Transaction amount in DT \\
2 & \code{hour} & Raw event & Hour of day (0--23) \\
3 & \code{is\_retrait} & Raw event & One-hot: operation is retrait \\
4 & \code{is\_versement} & Raw event & One-hot: operation is versement \\
5 & \code{is\_virement} & Raw event & One-hot: operation is virement \\
6 & \code{is\_cheque} & Raw event & One-hot: operation is remise de ch\`eques \\
7 & \code{is\_home\_branch} & Raw event & 1 if transaction at client's home branch \\
8 & \code{z\_amount} & Amount contrast & $(x - \mu_{30d})/\sigma_{30d}$ for this operation type \\
9 & \code{amount\_to\_mean\_ratio} & Amount contrast & $x/\mu_{30d}$ for this operation type \\
10 & \code{is\_above\_threshold} & Amount contrast & 1 if amount $\geq$ 10{,}000~DT \\
11 & \code{is\_near\_threshold} & Amount contrast & 1 if amount $\in$ [8{,}000, 9{,}999]~DT \\
12 & \code{is\_round\_amount} & Amount contrast & 1 if amount mod 1{,}000 $=$ 0 \\
13 & \code{is\_near\_cheque\_ceiling} & Amount contrast & 1 if cheque amount $\in$ [25{,}000, 29{,}999]~DT \\
14 & \code{op\_type\_probability} & Operation contrast & Client's 30d frequency ratio for this operation \\
15 & \code{is\_outside\_hours} & Timing contrast & 1 if hour $\notin$ [8, 16] \\
16 & \code{day\_distance\_from\_preferred} & Timing contrast & $|$day of month $-$ preferred day$|$ \\
17 & \code{is\_new\_counterparty} & Counterparty contrast & 1 if beneficiary/emitter not in known set \\
18 & \code{tx\_count\_24h} & Velocity & Client transactions in last 24 hours \\
19 & \code{tx\_count\_7d} & Velocity & Client transactions in last 7 days \\

22 & \code{near\_threshold\_count\_7d} & Velocity & Events in [8k, 10k)~DT band in last 7 days \\
23 & \code{employee\_tx\_count\_24h} & Employee & Employee's transaction count in last 24h \\
24 & \code{same\_employee\_client\_count\_24h} & Employee & Times this employee served this client in 24h \\
25 & \code{is\_salaried} & Context & One-hot: archetype is salaried \\
26 & \code{is\_small\_business} & Context & One-hot: archetype is small business \\
27 & \code{is\_student} & Context & One-hot: archetype is student \\
28 & \code{is\_retiree} & Context & One-hot: archetype is retiree \\
29 & \code{is\_big\_business} & Context & One-hot: archetype is big business \\
30 & \code{account\_age\_days} & Context & Days since account opening \\
\end{longtable}

Features 8--9 use a three-level fallback chain when statistics are unavailable for the current operation type: 30-day statistics, then 90-day, then the archetype baseline. Feature 13 applies only to cheque operations and is zero otherwise. Features 18--22 are computed from the Redis rolling buffer rather than the PostgreSQL profile, so they reflect the most recent events with sub-second latency.
